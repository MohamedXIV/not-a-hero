Little Nest — Blender 5.2.3

Editable, intentionally BLOCKY study of Yuna's underground home. Warm carved earth, plaster, woven rugs, bed, workbench, small water purifier, living plants and a 1.55 m Yuna proxy. These are the same room and orthographic camera azimuth at 4 elevations ABOVE horizontal: 90° would be vertical top-down. No 3D gameplay conversion.
