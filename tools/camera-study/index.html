Little Nest — Oblique Camera Study v2

Editable, intentionally BLOCKY study of Yuna's underground home. Warm carved earth, plaster, woven rugs, bed, workbench, small water purifier, living plants and a 1.55 m Yuna proxy. Corrected camera geometry: azimuth fixed at -90° (front-facing, axis-aligned walls; no diamond/isometric rotation). Compare ONLY the elevation at 70°, 60°, 50° and 40° ABOVE horizontal. At 90° the floor is seen straight down. Blender is previsualization only; game remains 2D.
